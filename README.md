My FreeCodeCamp.org assignment was to create a technical documentation page for a subject of my own choosing. I had already created a one-page app for practicing the "Criss-Cross" multiplication system for semi-mentally multiplying two-digit numbers times two-digit numbers, and the method requires typing the numbers backwards, from the lowest digit to the highest digit. So, for my assignment, I researched and presented different ways of typing numbers backwards and also included the latest version of my app and how to use it. I consider the app fun and addictive, and it has improved my facility with basic math and with my ability to work with different numbers in memory. The app is offered under the MIT license in the hope that someone will take up the torch of creating useful, simple and fun mental-math practiceware.
